Referencias de mood: documental medico de alto presupuesto, sala de monitoreo. Estructura: intro casi silenciosa, cuerpo con el pulso subiendo levemente hacia la cifra final, sin explotar en clímax. Duracion: loop de 2 a 3 minutos. Mood: clinico, tenso, preciso.

--- thumbnailBase ---
16:9, black background, [CAMBIAR: figura humana, organo o corte anatomico del tema, elegido segun lo que mas comunique el dato o limite del video] rendered in the channel's cinematic clinical-documentary visual language — near-black, graphite and bone-white palette, cold directional light, physically believable materials — with ONE physiological red accent marking the critical point only if narratively justified, half the frame left genuinely dark for the number and hook, no anatomical exposure forced if not needed, no gore. TENDENCIAS 2026: la cifra grande es el gancho de este canal — '-46°C', '11 DIAS', '8.000 m' leidos a 120px convierten mas que cualquier frase; el rojo se usa una sola vez por portada, solo cuando esta justificado; el negro puro destaca en el feed; evitar repetir siempre la misma composicion de figura humana abierta.

--- styleBase ---
LÍMITE HUMANO — VISUAL IDENTITY BIBLE

CHANNEL:
Clinical human biology, physiology, medicine, survival and scientifically documented limits of the human body.

CORE VISUAL LANGUAGE:
Premium cinematic science documentary. Dark, precise, restrained, clinically credible and visually mysterious. The image should feel like a frame from a high-end scientific documentary, never like a medical stock image, horror film, videogame, generic CGI render or fantasy artwork.

VISUAL CONSISTENCY:
Every scene must belong to the same visual universe, but scenes MUST NOT repeat the same composition, subject or anatomical cutaway. Consistency means identical visual language, not identical imagery.

COLOR:
Dominant near-black, graphite, charcoal, cold stone gray, bone white and desaturated blue-gray.
ONE controlled physiological red accent may appear only when narratively justified: circulation, cardiac activity, physiological stress, critical threshold or biological signal.
Red is never decorative and must never dominate the frame.
No orange cinematic grading, neon colors, saturated palettes or multicolor lighting.

LIGHT:
Cold directional cinematic key light, deep controlled shadows, high contrast, subtle cold rim light, selective illumination and restrained volumetric atmosphere.
The environment should remain predominantly dark.
Red may emit a small localized physiological glow only when justified.

MATERIALS:
Physically believable human skin with natural pores, subtle moisture and realistic imperfections.
Anatomical structures must look biologically credible and clinically precise.
Organs, vessels, muscle, tissue and fluids must never look like plastic, rubber, glossy CGI or fantasy creatures.

ANATOMICAL LANGUAGE:
Use anatomical visualization only when it communicates the narrative.
Anatomy may appear as:
- realistic anatomical exposure
- translucent anatomical overlay
- clean clinical cutaway
- isolated organ macro
- internal physiological visualization
- microscopic biological visualization

Never force anatomical exposure into every scene.

HUMAN SUBJECT:
When a human is required, use a consistent adult male subject, approximately 25–45 years old, natural athletic build, short dark hair and minimal dark technical clothing without logos.
Preserve identity whenever the same subject returns.
The subject is NOT mandatory in every scene.

COMPOSITION:
Cinematic vertical composition.
Use the most appropriate shot for the narrative:
macro, extreme close-up, close-up, medium, medium-wide, wide, extreme-wide, overhead, profile, silhouette or environmental composition.
Do not force centered symmetry in every scene.
Use negative space intentionally for post-production typography.

ENVIRONMENT:
Dark clinical spaces, controlled laboratory environments, natural extreme environments or near-black abstract space depending on the story.
The environment must support the scientific concept rather than distract from it.

TEXT:
NEVER generate text, letters, numbers, labels, subtitles, captions, typography or logos inside the image.
All text is added later in editing.

SAFETY / CLINICAL RESTRAINT:
No gore.
No graphic wounds.
No exposed reproductive anatomy.
No gratuitous blood.
No body horror.
No grotesque deformation.
Medical subjects should be represented clinically and scientifically.

NEGATIVE STYLE:
cartoon, anime, illustration, comic book, fantasy, superhero, videogame, horror, body horror, gore, graphic wounds, generic CGI, plastic skin, glossy organs, stock photography, bright hospital photography, colorful medical illustration, neon, oversaturated colors, orange teal grading, warm commercial lighting, text, typography, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, malformed anatomy, extra limbs, duplicated organs, floating organs, anatomical impossibilities.

--- animationBase ---
Treat the already-generated scene image as an IMMUTABLE MASTER FRAME: animation never redesigns identity, face, body, anatomy, wardrobe, environment, composition, lighting, color or materials — it only brings that exact frame to subtle life. A scene director selects ONE adaptable movement identity per shot depending on its narrative function and representation type, choosing from: minimal physiological movement for an organ (pulse, contraction, flow), natural micro-movement for a human subject (breathing, subtle posture shift), ambient environmental motion (fog, dust, snow, smoke, water, particles), progressive transparency reveal for anatomy (never morphing or deforming), extremely controlled micro-motion for macro shots, clean motion-graphic movement for scientific or graphic elements, gradual reveal for new information, an extremely slow push-in for suspense, justified higher-intensity movement only at a climax, and settling, stabilizing movement toward an ending. Camera uses exactly one primary movement per shot — slow push-in, slow pull-back, subtle lateral tracking, controlled orbit, slow reveal, locked camera or minimal parallax — never camera shake, fast zoom, random movement or impossible camera physics. Anatomical structures always stay connected and physically coherent: no morphing, melting, warping, floating organs, duplicated organs, face change, age change, body-proportion change, new limbs or missing limbs. If the frame contains editorial text, the text layer never animates, deforms or regenerates — movement happens behind or around it. Loopable, 5 to 8 seconds.

--- lineasRojas ---
No dar dosis, umbrales de medicacion ni informacion que sirva para autolesionarse. No convertir condiciones medicas reales en espectaculo. No mostrar gore ni imagenes de heridas: el estilo visual es diagramatico a proposito. Distinguir siempre caso documentado, estimacion y modelo teorico. En temas de sueno, ayuno o frio extremo, advertir explicitamente que no son retos para replicar.

--- tiempos (7) ---

• La pregunta retorica que sube la apuesta: cuanto crees que aguantarias tu.
